Painter Website Overview:

1. 10-Page Customizable Website:

This website is specifically designed for painting businesses. It comes with 10 fully customizable pages, allowing you to tailor the content to suit your business needs.

The website uses Custom Values to automatically populate important business information, such as your company name, contact information, and service details, making it efficient to update once and reflect everywhere on the site.

2. Key Pages Included:

Home Page: A professional introduction to your painting business, highlighting key services and client testimonials.

Residential & Commercial Services Pages: Detailed pages showcasing the types of painting services your business offers.

Consultation Booking Page: Allows potential customers to easily schedule a painting consultation using an integrated calendar system.

Portfolio Page: Showcase completed projects to build trust with prospective clients.

Contact Page: Includes contact forms and auto-populated business info, encouraging visitors to reach out.

3. Easy-to-Use Features:

The website is built to be user-friendly with a focus on converting visitors into leads.

Integrates with your GoHighLevel system to automate booking consultations and capture free estimate requests directly through the website.

This snapshot is designed to streamline your business and enhance your online presence, making it easier to manage customer inquiries, showcase services, and secure more appointments.

Funnel Documentation:

This funnel is designed to offer a $100 discount to new leads and customers. It captures potential clients through a lead form or a landing page.

Once a lead submits their information, they are automatically funneled into the Main Painter Pipeline, where their status is tracked and updated based on interactions.

Once the form is completed, the lead is placed into the Main Painter Pipeline, where automation helps qualify the lead and trigger actions such as follow-up messages.

This funnel is focused on offering a variety of coupons that customers can redeem for painting services.

Leads enter the Main Painter Pipeline once they opt into receiving coupons, and automation handles sending them the specific discount along with follow-up instructions.

This funnel offers a free color consultation to attract potential customers who are unsure about their painting needs.

Abandoned Estimate Follow-Up Automation:

This follow-up automation is designed to engage leads who have received an estimate but have not yet proceeded with the booking. The automation sends a series of reminders through emails and SMS over a set period to keep the lead engaged and encourage them to take the next step.

Key Features:

The automation sends reminders at different intervals after the estimate is provided:

Email 1 is sent 3 days after the estimate.

Reminder SMS is sent 6 days after the estimate.

Email 2 is sent 7 days after the estimate.

Email 3 is sent 14 days after the estimate.

Final SMS reminder is sent 2 days after Email 3.

Replied Automation:

When a lead responds to the follow-up messages in the Abandoned Estimate Follow-Up Automation, they are automatically removed from the sequence, preventing further follow-up reminders from being sent. This action is managed by the Replied Automation, which:

Removes the lead from the workflow once they reply.

Creates or updates the opportunity in the pipeline.

Sends an internal notification to your team, alerting them of the reply with the lead’s details and next steps.

This ensures that your follow-up process is smooth and efficient, and no unnecessary messages are sent to leads who have already re-engaged.

8. Google Review Campaign:

This Google Review Campaign is designed to gather feedback from satisfied clients and boost your online reputation through automated follow-up requests for Google reviews. The campaign ensures that clients who have had a great experience with your painting business can easily leave a review, and it tracks their interaction with the review request.

Key Features:

Initial SMS Request:

An SMS is sent to the client, thanking them for their business and requesting a Google review.

The message includes a link to the business’s Google review page using the placeholder: {{custom_values.google_review_link}}.

This message is followed by two more SMS reminders and emails at intervals if the client does not take immediate action.

Reminder Sequence:

If the client does not leave a review after the first SMS, the system waits a few days and sends follow-up reminders.

These reminders, both via SMS and email, encourage the client to click the review link and share their feedback.

Review Link Clicked:

When a client clicks on the Google review link, a trigger activates the Review Campaign Link Clicked Workflow.

This workflow sends an internal notification to your team, informing them that the client has clicked the review link.

It updates the lead or opportunity status in the pipeline and removes the client from the review campaign workflow to prevent additional reminders.

A tag is applied to the contact to indicate they have clicked the review link, allowing easy tracking of review interaction.

Internal Notification:

The internal notification provides the team with key details, such as the contact’s name, and prompts them to follow up with the client to express gratitude for leaving a review (if one was posted).

This personalized touch helps reinforce a positive relationship with the client.

The Google Review Campaign ensures that clients are gently reminded to leave a review and that your team is notified as soon as the review link is clicked. This automation helps build your online reputation while keeping the process smooth and efficient for both the business and the client.

10 Missed Call Text Back Automations:

Basic Missed Call Text Back Automation:

This automation is triggered when a call is missed by your team.

The system sends an immediate SMS to the missed caller with a message like:

“Hey there, this is {{user.first_name}} from {{location.name}}. We just got a missed call from this number. Sorry about that! How can I help?”

The contact is tagged as a missed call, and an internal notification is sent to your team, prompting follow-up actions.

Advanced Missed Call Text Back Sequence:

This automation handles multiple missed calls with more detailed follow-up processes.

After each missed call, the system triggers a series of follow-ups, depending on business hours:

During Business Hours: The system sends an SMS immediately and notifies your team.

Outside Business Hours: The SMS is delayed until the start of the next business day.

If the caller continues to miss calls, additional follow-ups are triggered. For example:

On the second missed call, another SMS is sent, and internal notifications are triggered.

On the third missed call, the contact is tagged, and the system initiates another follow-up SMS and internal notification, depending on the time of day.

The sequence adjusts based on whether it’s business hours or not, ensuring leads receive timely responses.

These automations ensure that missed calls are immediately addressed, keeping communication smooth and preventing potential leads from slipping through the cracks.
