1. Website Overview

The Cleaning Business Website included in the snapshot is designed to provide a professional, conversion-optimized online presence for your cleaning business. The website comes pre-built with key pages, and any business-specific information will auto-populate using custom values, ensuring quick and easy customization without needing to edit individual elements manually.

2. Key Features of the Website

Customizable Design: All sections of the website can be easily modified to fit your branding, messaging, and services. Whether you want to add your logo, adjust the color scheme, or update content, this snapshot allows full flexibility.

Auto-Populated Custom Values: Fields such as business name, phone number, website URL, and service-specific details will automatically pull from the custom values you set in GoHighLevel, ensuring that your website is ready to go live without needing manual updates.

Pre-Built Pages: The snapshot includes essential pages for any cleaning business:

Home Page: Designed to give a professional first impression, showcasing your cleaning services, client testimonials, and a prominent call to action for booking.

Contact Us Page: Includes a contact form that allows leads to easily get in touch with your business. All submissions will be stored in the CRM for follow-up.

Thank You Page: A post-contact page that thanks customers for reaching out and informs them of next steps. This page can also serve as a confirmation page for appointments.

About Us Page: Provides an overview of your cleaning business and its mission. This page can be customized with your company’s history, values, and team information.

Residential Cleaning Services Page: Lists detailed descriptions of your home cleaning services, emphasizing the value and professionalism of your business.

Move Cleaning Page: Specifically tailored to moving-in/moving-out cleaning services, highlighting how your company can ease the stress of moving with thorough clean-ups.

Deep Cleaning Page: Focuses on in-depth cleaning services, perfect for customers seeking a more comprehensive clean.

Office Cleaning Page: Aimed at commercial clients, showcasing your office cleaning services and highlighting the importance of a clean workplace.

Privacy Policy: Pre-built with placeholder text that can easily be customized to fit your company’s privacy guidelines.

Terms of Service: Includes a professional terms of service agreement that is essential for any business. This page also mentions that no refunds can be offered due to the downloadable nature of the services (applicable to digital snapshots or service agreements).

3. Custom Values for Website Personalization

Phone Number: {{ location.phone }}

The website is built with mobile responsiveness in mind, ensuring that your site looks and functions great on all devices, from smartphones to desktops.

3. Funnels Overview

The Cleaning Business Snapshot includes five fully automated funnels designed to help you generate leads, capture bookings, and offer special promotions. Each funnel is linked to the Cleaning Pipeline that tracks leads from inquiry to service completion, ensuring no lead slips through the cracks.

Steps:

Landing Page: Customers enter their details to claim the $100 discount.

Thank You Page: Confirmation of the offer with next steps to book.

Automation: Once the form is submitted, the customer will receive an SMS and an email confirming the discount and prompting them to schedule a service.

Pipeline Updates: When a new lead claims the discount, their details are automatically added to the Cleaning Pipeline, starting at the "New Lead" stage.

2. Carpet Cleaning Funnel

Purpose: This funnel is tailored for promoting your carpet cleaning services.

Steps:

Landing Page: A professional page showcasing the benefits of carpet cleaning services with a form to schedule a consultation.

Confirmation Page: After submitting, leads are prompted to schedule their cleaning or receive a quote.

Automation: SMS and email follow-ups will guide the lead through the booking process.

Pipeline Updates: Leads who inquire about carpet cleaning will automatically move through the pipeline based on their booking progress.

3. Free Quote Funnel

Purpose: Designed to generate leads by offering a free quote for any cleaning service.

Steps:

Landing Page: The lead fills out a form requesting a free quote.

Thank You Page: Confirmation of their inquiry, with instructions on the next steps.

Automation: The customer will receive an SMS and email confirming their quote request and prompting them to schedule a follow-up call or consultation.

Pipeline Updates: Leads requesting quotes will be tagged as "Hot Lead" and move through the pipeline as they progress towards booking.

4. Maid Service Special Funnel

Purpose: This funnel promotes your maid service with a special offer.

Steps:

Landing Page: Highlights the special offer for maid services, encouraging visitors to sign up for the deal.

Thank You Page: Confirmation of the special with a prompt to schedule a cleaning service.

Automation: SMS and email notifications encourage the lead to complete their booking.

Pipeline Updates: Leads entering the maid service special are tracked in the pipeline, moving from "New Lead" to "Appointment Booked" as they schedule their service.

5. Recurring Service Discount Funnel

Purpose: Promote discounted rates for customers who sign up for recurring cleaning services (e.g., weekly or bi-weekly).

Pipeline for All Funnels: Cleaning Pipeline

Each funnel is linked to the Cleaning Pipeline, which has the following stages:

New Lead: A lead has entered the funnel but hasn't yet booked.

Hot Lead: The lead has shown interest by filling out a form or claiming an offer.

Appointment Booked: The lead has scheduled a cleaning service.

Appointment No Show: If the lead misses an appointment, this status is updated.

Appointment Canceled: Leads who cancel are moved to this stage.

Service Completed: Once the service is successfully provided, the lead is marked as completed.

Follow-Up: If further follow-up is needed, the system will move the lead here for future engagement.

Automations for Each Funnel

All five funnels are fully automated, ensuring that once a lead enters the funnel, they receive:

SMS reminders and emails at each stage to keep them engaged and on track to book a service.

Internal notifications for your team to follow up as needed, ensuring timely responses.

Custom tags applied to leads based on their funnel entry, which helps with segmentation and future marketing efforts.

By integrating automated funnels with the Cleaning Pipeline, you'll have a streamlined system that tracks every lead’s journey from initial contact to service completion, increasing efficiency and improving conversion rates.

4. Appointment Workflows Overview

The Cleaning Business Snapshot includes four key appointment-related workflows to ensure smooth communication and management of your cleaning appointments. These workflows handle appointment reminders, no-shows, cancellations, and service completions, ensuring your customers stay informed and engaged throughout their experience with your business.

1. Appointment Reminder Workflow (Cleaning Service)

Purpose: Automatically sends reminders to customers about their upcoming cleaning appointments, ensuring they are prepared and reducing the chances of missed appointments.

Triggers:

When an appointment is booked.

Reminder notifications are sent 24 hours and 1 hour before the scheduled appointment.

Actions:

SMS Reminder: A text message is sent to remind the customer of their upcoming service, including the appointment date and time.

Example Message: "Hi {{ contact.first_name }}! 🎉 Your cleaning service with {{ location.name }} is confirmed for {{ appointment.only_start_date }} at {{ appointment.only_start_time }}. We can’t wait to make your space spotless! 🧼✨"

Email Reminder: An email is also sent to provide further details and allow customers to reschedule if needed.

Benefits: Reduces no-shows and helps ensure customers are ready for their service.

2. Appointment No-Show Workflow (Cleaning Service)

Purpose: Automatically manages situations where a customer misses their scheduled cleaning appointment, providing options to reschedule.

Triggers:

When the appointment status is updated to "No Show."

Actions:

SMS Follow-Up: Sends a message to the customer informing them that they missed their appointment and offering to reschedule.

Example Message: "Hi {{ contact.first_name }}, we noticed you missed your scheduled cleaning appointment with {{ location.name }} today. 😔 If you'd like to reschedule, please reply to this message or call us at {{ location.phone }} to find a new time that works for you. We’re happy to get your cleaning service back on track!"

Internal Notification: Your team receives an internal notification, allowing them to follow up manually if needed.

Email: An email is also sent with similar rescheduling options.

Benefits: Ensures no missed appointments are left unaddressed, increasing the chances of retaining customers.

3. Appointment Canceled Workflow (Cleaning Service)

Purpose: This workflow handles situations where a customer cancels their appointment, allowing them to easily reschedule and keeping your team informed.

Triggers:

When the appointment status is updated to "Canceled."

Actions:

SMS Confirmation: Sends a message confirming the cancellation and providing rescheduling options.

Example Message: "Hi {{ contact.first_name }}, your cleaning appointment with {{ location.name }} on {{ appointment.only_start_date }} at {{ appointment.only_start_time }} has been successfully canceled. If you’d like to reschedule, please contact us at {{ location.phone }}. We’d love to assist you when you’re ready!"

Internal Notification: Notifies your team of the cancellation for manual follow-up if necessary.

Email: Sends a cancellation confirmation email to the customer.

Benefits: Allows for seamless communication with customers after cancellations and provides opportunities to rebook.

4. Appointment Completed Workflow (Cleaning Service)

Purpose: Automatically manages completed appointments by updating the status in your system and ensuring the customer has received all necessary communications.

Triggers:

When an appointment status is updated to "Completed."

Actions:

Remove Tags: Removes any tags related to the booking process (e.g., "Appointment Booked") to keep customer records clean.

Create or Update Opportunity: Updates the lead's opportunity status in your pipeline to reflect the completed service.

Benefits: Helps keep your CRM and pipeline clean and organized, ensuring accurate reporting and follow-ups for completed services.

Workflow Automations and Benefits

Each of these workflows is fully automated, ensuring seamless communication with your customers at every stage of the appointment process. Key benefits include:

Reduced No-Shows: Through timely reminders and follow-ups.

Improved Rescheduling: By providing easy ways for customers to reschedule missed or canceled appointments.

Increased Customer Satisfaction: By keeping customers informed and engaged throughout their experience.

Efficient Team Management: With internal notifications, your team will always stay informed about the status of each appointment.

By integrating these appointment workflows into your cleaning business operations, you ensure that every appointment is managed effectively, improving customer satisfaction and streamlining your business processes.

5. 52-Week Nurture Campaigns Overview

The Cleaning Business Snapshot includes two fully automated, year-long nurture campaigns designed to keep leads engaged and gradually convert them into loyal customers. These campaigns share educational content and cleaning tips on a regular basis, helping you stay top-of-mind for potential clients while providing valuable insights.

1. 52-Week Email Nurture Campaign (Educational)

Purpose: This year-long email campaign is designed to educate leads and prospects about various aspects of cleaning, offering valuable tips and highlighting your services along the way. The goal is to build a relationship with leads by consistently providing value, eventually guiding them toward booking your services.

Structure:

Frequency: One email per week for 52 weeks.

Content: Each email provides useful cleaning tips, seasonal cleaning advice, or ways to maintain a cleaner home or office. It also subtly promotes your services, encouraging leads to take the next step.

CTAs: Call-to-actions such as "Book Now," "Reply Yes," or "Visit our Website" are rotated throughout the campaign to prompt engagement without being overly sales-focused.

Automation:

Each email is scheduled automatically after the lead opts in.

Emails are customized with dynamic placeholders like {{ contact.first_name }} and {{ location.phone }} to ensure personalization.

Benefits:

Keeps your cleaning business top-of-mind all year round.

Builds trust and credibility by providing consistent value.

Gradually nurtures leads into customers with well-timed offers.

2. 52-Week Text Message (SMS) Nurture Campaign (Educational)

Purpose: Similar to the email campaign, the SMS nurture campaign focuses on educating leads but in a more concise, mobile-friendly format. This campaign is designed to deliver bite-sized tips and value directly to your leads' phones on a weekly basis.

Structure:

Frequency: One SMS per week for 52 weeks.

Content: Each message includes a short cleaning tip or seasonal advice, followed by a CTA prompting the recipient to take action (e.g., book a cleaning service, reply to the message, visit your website).

CTAs: The calls to action rotate between "Reply Yes," "Call Us at {{ location.phone }}," and "Visit Our Website {{ location.website }}," depending on the message content.

Automation:

Each SMS is automatically sent every week.

The campaign leverages dynamic placeholders (e.g., {{ contact.first_name }}) to add a personal touch.

Benefits:

Provides immediate value with concise, actionable tips.

Leverages SMS for higher open and engagement rates compared to email.

Keeps your business in regular contact with leads while gradually encouraging them to book services.

Customization

Both the Email and SMS Nurture Campaigns can be customized to fit your business’s unique style, branding, and messaging. You can easily:

Update the content of each email or SMS.

Change the frequency of messages if needed.

Tailor the CTA based on your latest promotions or offers.

Personalization with Custom Values

The campaigns use the following custom values to dynamically personalize messages:

{{ contact.first_name }}: To address the lead by their first name.

{{ location.name }}: For your business name.

{{ location.phone }}: For your business contact number.

{{ location.website }}: For directing leads to your website.

By maintaining ongoing communication with your leads through these campaigns, you increase the likelihood of converting them into paying customers while offering them consistent value and insights into maintaining a clean and healthy environment.

These 52-week nurture campaigns are designed to work in tandem with your lead generation efforts, ensuring that no lead is left unattended and that you have regular touchpoints with prospects throughout the year.

Google Review Campaign Workflow

Purpose: To prompt customers to leave a Google review after they’ve received a cleaning service.

Automation Steps:

SMS 1: Sent after the service is completed, requesting feedback.

Example SMS: "Hi {{ contact.first_name }}! We hope you enjoyed your experience with {{ location.name }}. 🧼✨ We’d love to hear your feedback! Please take a moment to leave us a Google review: {{ custom_values.google_review_link }}. Thank you for your support!"

Follow-Up: Additional reminders are sent via SMS and email over the next few days if no action is taken.

Pipeline Updates: The lead’s journey through the Google Review Campaign Pipeline is tracked automatically, allowing you to monitor the effectiveness of the campaign.

Review Campaign Pipeline Stages

The Google Review Campaign Pipeline includes the following stages to track customer interactions:

Request Sent: The initial review request has been sent to the customer.

Review Link Clicked: The customer clicked the link to leave a review, indicating engagement.

Review Link NOT Clicked: The customer has not clicked the link, and follow-up messages will continue to be sent.

This pipeline helps you gauge which customers are responding to your review requests and which ones may need additional follow-up.

Follow-Up Workflow: Review Link Clicked

If the customer clicks on the review link, an additional automation is triggered:

Internal Notification: Your team will receive an internal notification to inform them that the customer clicked on the Google review link, allowing for further follow-up if necessary.

Example Notification: "{{ contact.first_name }} clicked on your GMB Review Link! They may have left a review, so make sure to thank them!"

This internal communication ensures that your team can personally follow up and thank the customer for leaving a review, adding a personal touch to the interaction.

Benefits of the Google Review Campaign

Increased Reviews: By automating the review request process, you’re more likely to receive consistent feedback from satisfied customers.

Improved Reputation: Positive Google reviews can significantly improve your business’s online visibility and credibility.

Automation Saves Time: You won’t need to manually request reviews, as the system automatically handles sending reminders and follow-ups based on customer actions.

Pipeline Tracking: You can easily see which customers have clicked on the review link and who may need additional prompting.

This campaign helps you maintain an active presence on Google while leveraging satisfied customers to help grow your business through positive online reviews.

10. Payment Automations Documentation

1. Initial Payment Request After Service Completion

This automation sends a payment request to the client after the service has been completed. It follows up with both email and SMS reminders to ensure the client receives the invoice and can make payment in a timely manner.

Automation Trigger:

Service status changes to "Service Completed."

Workflow Steps:

Trigger:

This automation is triggered once the service is marked as completed in the system.

Email 1:

Sends an email with the invoice details, including the amount due, due date, and payment link.

Example Message: Hi {{contact.first_name}}! 🧹 Your invoice for {{invoice.amount}} is ready and due by {{invoice.due_date}}. Click here to make your payment: {{invoice.url}}. If you have any questions, feel free to call us at {{location.phone}}. Thanks for choosing {{location.name}}!

SMS 1:

Sends a similar message via SMS to ensure clients who may not check their email receive the invoice.

Example SMS Message: Hi {{contact.first_name}}! 🧹 Your invoice for {{invoice.amount}} is ready and due by {{invoice.due_date}}. Click here to make your payment: {{invoice.url}}. Feel free to call us at {{location.phone}} if you have any questions.

Wait 2 Days:

Waits 2 days, then sends a follow-up reminder.

Email 2:

Sends a second reminder email if the payment has not yet been made.

SMS 2:

Sends a second SMS with the same reminder details.

Wait 3 Days:

Waits another 3 days, and repeats the follow-up sequence.

Internal Notification:

After 7 days of non-payment, an internal notification is sent to your team to follow up with the client.

2. Payment Received

This automation confirms receipt of payment once the invoice has been paid and sends a thank-you message to the client.

Automation Trigger:

Invoice status changes to "Paid."

Workflow Steps:

Trigger:

This automation is triggered when the payment is successfully processed and marked as paid in the system.

Remove from Workflow:

Removes the client from the "Initial Payment Request" workflow to stop further payment reminders.

Thank You Email:

Sends a thank-you email to the client acknowledging receipt of payment.

Example Message: Thank you for your payment, {{contact.first_name}}! We appreciate your business. If you have any questions or feedback, please feel free to reach out to us at {{location.phone}}.

Thank You SMS:

Sends a similar thank-you message via SMS.

Example SMS: Thank you for your payment, {{contact.first_name}}! Let us know if you have any questions!

Wait 2 Days:

Waits 2 days after payment confirmation.

Feedback Email & SMS:

After 2 days, the system sends a request for feedback, encouraging the client to provide feedback on the service provided.

3. Refund

This automation informs the client when a refund has been processed and provides an estimated timeframe for when the client will receive the funds.

Automation Trigger:

Invoice status changes to "Refund."

Workflow Steps:
