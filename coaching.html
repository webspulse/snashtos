Step 1: Importing a Snapshot

Importing from a Shared Snapshot Link

Log into your GoHighLevel account.

Open a new browser tab and paste the shared link.

Click Yes! Import Now to confirm.

Importing from Email

Log into GoHighLevel.

Open the email and click the import link.

Click Yes! Import Now to complete the process.

Step 2: Setting Up Your Domain

Log in to your GoHighLevel account and navigate to Settings > Domains.

Click Add Domain and enter your domain name.

The system will configure the DNS settings for Google Domains, GoDaddy, or Cloudflare automatically.

If using an unsupported provider, follow the provided DNS setup instructions.

Step 14: Included Automations
