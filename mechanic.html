Website Automations

The Mechanic Website includes three key website automations that ensure every lead is properly managed and tracked through the Marketing Pipeline. Each automation is designed to streamline communication and ensure no lead is missed.

Chat Widget Automation

Trigger: Customer replies via the chat widget on the website.

Workflow: The system tags the lead as a "chat widget lead" and creates a new lead in the Marketing Pipeline. The internal team is notified, and the lead receives an automated SMS thanking them for reaching out and providing contact information for further assistance.

Purpose: To ensure leads coming through the chat widget are instantly acknowledged and nurtured.

Live Chat Automation

Trigger: Customer initiates a conversation through live chat.

Workflow: Similar to the chat widget automation, leads are tagged and added to the pipeline. The internal team is alerted, and the lead receives an SMS confirmation of their inquiry with follow-up contact details.

Purpose: To provide an immediate response and assign every lead coming in through live chat to the proper pipeline for follow-up.

Website Form Submission Automation

Trigger: Customer submits a form on the website.

Workflow: Leads who submit the form are tagged and marked as new leads in the Marketing Pipeline. An internal notification is sent to the team, and the lead receives an SMS confirming the receipt of their request, along with business contact information.

Purpose: To ensure every form submission is captured and responded to in a timely manner, helping increase conversion rates by following up quickly.

Each of these automations works seamlessly with the Marketing Pipeline, which organizes leads through stages such as New Lead, Hot Lead, New Booking, Visit Attended, and more. This pipeline ensures no lead is left behind and that each stage of the customer journey is managed effectively.

5. Appointment Workflows

The Mechanic Snapshot includes four key appointment workflows that streamline the customer experience from booking to follow-up. Each workflow ensures that appointments are properly managed, reducing no-shows and cancellations while improving customer communication. These workflows integrate with the Marketing Pipeline to keep track of lead statuses and trigger appropriate automations based on appointment outcomes.

Appointment Confirmation + Reminders

Trigger: When an appointment is confirmed, this workflow sends a confirmation email and follow-up reminders.

Workflow: The lead receives an email confirming their appointment with {{appointment.only_start_date}} and {{appointment.timezone}} details. Reminder emails and SMS messages are sent 24 hours and 1 hour before the scheduled appointment.

Goal: To confirm appointments and reduce missed appointments by sending timely reminders.

Appointment No Show

Trigger: When an appointment is marked as a "No Show."

Workflow: This workflow removes any booked tags, adds a "No Show" tag, and triggers an internal notification. The customer receives a follow-up SMS with a link to reschedule their appointment, followed by an additional SMS reminder after one day if they haven't rescheduled.

Goal: To re-engage customers who missed their appointment and prompt them to reschedule.

Appointment Canceled

Trigger: When an appointment is canceled.

Workflow: The workflow removes the "Booked" tag, adds an "Appointment Canceled" tag, and sends a follow-up SMS to the customer with a link to reschedule. If the customer does not respond, a second SMS is sent one day later to remind them to reschedule.

Goal: To recover canceled appointments and provide an easy way for customers to reschedule.

Appointment Completed

Trigger: When an appointment is marked as completed.

Workflow: This workflow removes the relevant tags, updates the lead’s status in the pipeline as "Visit Attended," and removes the contact from all active workflows.

Goal: To conclude the customer’s appointment experience and update the pipeline for accurate follow-up actions.

These appointment workflows work together to improve customer engagement and ensure that no appointment is forgotten or missed. Each workflow can be easily customized to fit your mechanic business's specific needs.

7. Google Review Campaign

The Google Review Campaign is designed to collect reviews from customers who have recently used your services. This campaign automates the process of sending review requests through SMS and email, ensuring maximum engagement and feedback. The pipeline keeps track of each stage to help monitor who has responded and who needs follow-up communication.

Stages in the Google Review Campaign Pipeline:

Request Sent: When the first review request is sent to the customer.

Link Clicked: When the customer clicks the provided link to leave a review.

Not Clicked: When the customer has not clicked the link, and follow-up messages are necessary.

Workflow for the Google Review Campaign:

Trigger: The campaign is triggered when a customer is tagged with "Review Request Sent."

SMS 1: An SMS is sent to the customer asking for a quick review, with a link to leave feedback: "{{custom_values.google_review_campaign_link}}."

Email 1: An email is sent with the same request and link, providing another opportunity for the customer to leave a review.

Follow-Ups: If no action is taken, additional SMS and email reminders are sent after one day, and again after five days, encouraging the customer to leave a review.

Completion: Once the link is clicked, the pipeline is updated to "Link Clicked," and no further follow-up is sent.

Monitoring Progress:

The system automatically updates the status of the lead based on their interaction with the review request. If the customer clicks the link, the system marks it as "Link Clicked" and removes the customer from the workflow. If the customer doesn't click the link, they stay in the "Not Clicked" stage, and follow-up messages continue.

This campaign simplifies the process of gathering customer reviews while ensuring that no opportunity is missed. The automation works behind the scenes, continuously prompting customers to provide feedback, which can improve your Google review ratings and enhance your business reputation.

13. Customer Referral Campaign

The Customer Referral Campaign in the Mechanic Snapshot is designed to encourage existing customers to refer their friends and family, helping to generate new leads and build customer loyalty. This automation rewards customers for successful referrals, fostering a mutually beneficial relationship.

Trigger: The workflow is triggered when a customer's opportunity status is marked as "Won," indicating that they have completed a service and are now eligible for referral offers.

Workflow:

Step 1: After a 7-day delay, the system sends an SMS to the customer thanking them for their loyalty and offering a referral incentive. The message reads:

"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, we appreciate your loyalty to {{location.name}}! As a thank you, we're offering {{custom_values.referral_campaign_offer}} when you refer a friend. Have someone in mind? Reply to this message or call {{location.phone}} to get started!"

Step 2: The system waits 1 day to check if the customer responds. If they reply, an Internal Notification is sent to the team to follow up and manage the referral process.

Step 3: If no response is received, the system sends a follow-up SMS and waits another day.

Step 4: The system checks again for a reply, and if the customer shows interest, the team is notified to proceed with the referral. If there is no response, the campaign ends.

Purpose: To incentivize current customers to refer others, helping to grow your customer base organically. The automation keeps customers engaged and rewards them for their loyalty, creating a win-win situation for both the business and the customer.

This referral campaign makes it easy for customers to refer their friends, boosting word-of-mouth marketing and generating new leads for your mechanic business.
