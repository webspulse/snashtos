Step 1: Importing a Snapshot

From a Share Link:

Log into your platform account.

Open an additional browser tab.

Paste the snapshot share link and press enter.

Click the "Import Now" button.

From an Email:

Log into your platform account.

Click the provided link in the email.

Click the "Import Now" button.

Step 2: Domain Setup

Features:

Seamless integration with providers like Google Domains, GoDaddy, or Cloudflare.

Automatic DNS configuration.

Steps:

Log into your sub-account and go to Settings > Domains.

Click Add Domain.

Enter your domain name or subdomain and click Continue.

Verify and test the connection.

Step 3: Phone System Setup

Configuring Twilio or LeadConnector:

Access Settings > Phone System.

Update credentials or integrate with Twilio using account-specific details.

You can customize call flows and assign numbers for specific campaigns to optimize call tracking.

Step 4: Email System Setup

LeadConnector Email or Mailgun:

Set up an account with Mailgun or LeadConnector.

Verify the domain and add DNS records as needed.

Test email configurations for proper delivery.

Email templates and sending behavior can be customized to align with branding or campaign goals.

Step 5: Third-Party Account Integrations

Gmail, Stripe, and Facebook:

Navigate to Settings > Integrations.

Follow the connection prompts for each account type.

Enter login credentials when prompted.

Each integration can be tailored for specific purposes, such as processing payments through Stripe or syncing emails with Gmail.

Step 6: Editing Custom Values

Custom values can be updated to automatically reflect across workflows, pages, and settings. Examples include:

Business name, phone number, and address.

Social media links and website URLs.

Branding elements like logo and color schemes.

Custom values allow for one-time edits that reflect universally, saving time and maintaining consistency.

Step 7: Adding Trigger Links

Go to Marketing > Trigger Links.

Add custom links, such as Google or Facebook review links.

Use these links in workflows for tracking or engagement purposes.

These links can be further personalized with placeholders or action-based tracking.

Step 8: Website Branding

Open the Sites tab and select your website.

Customize elements such as colors, logos, and layout using the website builder.

You can edit pages to add personalized content, videos, and call-to-action buttons.

Add or remove sections as needed to reflect your services or promotions.

Step 9: Calendar Settings

Configure appointment settings, including:

Slot duration and intervals.

Buffer times and maximum daily appointments.

Minimum scheduling notice and date range.

Calendars can be branded and synchronized with team schedules, ensuring smooth appointment management.

Step 10: Automations

Editing Workflows:

Go to the Automation tab.

Select workflows and modify text or triggers as required.

You can add conditions or actions to fine-tune how workflows respond to user behavior.

Adding Actions:

Use the Add New Action button to create new steps in workflows.

Actions like email sends, SMS sends, and task assignments can be tailored for various scenarios.

Step 11: Funnels and Websites Included

The snapshot includes the following customizable assets, all easily editable using custom values to reflect your branding and business needs:

Customer Support Window:

Funnel steps include a Support Ticket Page, Ticket Thank You Page, and Support Call Page.

You can adjust text and design to match your business support structure.

Sales Funnels:

Lead Magnet Funnel: Ideal for capturing leads with a downloadable offer or resource.

VSL Funnel: Perfect for sharing a Video Sales Letter to convert leads.

Both funnels can be edited to include custom offers or testimonials.

4-Step Onboarding Funnel:

Steps include a Payment Page, Onboarding Portal, Success Call, and Welcome page.

Personalize these steps to introduce clients to your services and processes.

Reputation Management:

Feedback Survey: Collect client feedback to improve services.

Positive Review Funnel: Encourage satisfied clients to leave reviews.

Update these workflows with personalized thank-you messages or rewards.

Linktree Options:

Multiple versions include Services Linktree, Tools & Resources Linktree, Grow With Us Linktree, and Stay Connected Linktree.

Adjust links to direct clients to your most important resources.

Digital Marketing Websites:

Version 1 and Version 2: Customizable websites tailored for digital marketing services.

Update elements like logos, colors, and text effortlessly with custom values.

Each component is pre-built but can be easily tailored to fit your specific business needs, making the setup process efficient and straightforward.

Step 12: Automated Pipelines Included

The snapshot includes the following pipelines, designed to automate lead management and streamline operations:

Main Pipeline:

Tracks leads through stages like New Lead, Intro Call, Demo Booked, No Show, Cancelled, Rescheduled, and Not Interested.

These stages can be renamed or re-ordered to fit your sales process.

Outreach Pipeline:

Manages outreach efforts with stages such as Campaign Started, Call 1, Call 2, Call 3, No Answer, Follow Up, and Not Interested.

Customize call scripts or SMS follow-ups for each stage.

Support Tickets Pipeline:

Handles client support requests with stages like New Ticket, In Progress, and Closed.

Edit these stages to include escalations or priority levels.

Onboarding Funnel Pipeline:

Guides clients through onboarding stages, including Paid, Onboarding Form Completed, Success Call Booked, Success Call Cancelled, No Show, and Onboarding Completed (Review Pending).

Tailor the stages to match specific onboarding workflows.

Database Reactivation (DR) Campaign Pipeline:

Focuses on re-engaging past clients with stages such as Offer Sent, Interested, and Not Interested.

Update stages to include incentives or expiration reminders.

Each pipeline is pre-configured but can be adjusted to match your specific business processes, ensuring a seamless integration into your operations.
