4. Lead Management Process for Funnels and Website

In the Pet Grooming GoHighLevel Snapshot, all leads that come through the funnels or website will be automatically directed into the Main Pipeline. This pipeline is structured to manage the lead flow efficiently, ensuring that every stage of the customer journey is captured and followed up appropriately.

Here’s a breakdown of the pipeline stages:

New Lead: This is where all incoming leads from the website or funnels are initially placed.

Lead Responded: When the lead engages, responds, or shows interest, they are moved to this stage.

Online Purchase: If the lead completes an online purchase (such as booking a grooming session or purchasing a package), they are updated to this stage.

Appt Booked: Leads who successfully book an appointment are moved here.

Appt Canceled: If an appointment is canceled, the lead is placed in this stage for follow-up.

Appt No Show: If a lead doesn't show up for their scheduled appointment, they are tagged here for future follow-up.

Follow Up: Any leads needing additional outreach, reminders, or nurturing are placed in this stage to ensure nothing is missed.

With the visibility features enabled for funnel and pie charts, the pipeline ensures that the status of each lead is tracked and easily viewed, allowing for detailed reporting and continuous optimization of the sales process.

10. Missed Call Text Back

Basic Missed Call Text Back Workflow:

Trigger:

Initiates when a call status is missed.

Branches:

If the contact's first name field is empty (i.e., new contact), no further action.

Otherwise, the workflow continues.

Actions:

Tag: Adds a "Missed Call" tag to the contact.

SMS: Sends an SMS to the contact saying: Hey there, this is {{user.first_name}} from {{location.name}}, we just got a missed call from this number, sorry about that! How can I help?

Internal Notification: Sends an internal notification to the team.

Advanced Missed Call Text Back Sequence:

Trigger:

Initiates when a call status is missed.

Branches:

If the contact has already received a missed call tag, the workflow repeats the text back actions.

If the contact is new or hasn't missed a call before, it proceeds with initial outreach.

Actions:

Tag: Adds a "Missed Call" tag to the contact.

SMS Sequence:

1st SMS: Acknowledges the missed call with a similar message as the basic workflow.

2nd SMS (If missed again): Sends a follow-up message: Sorry, we've missed your call for the 2nd time. How can we help you?

Internal Notification: Sends internal notifications to the team at each missed call checkpoint.

Branching Logic: Depending on whether the missed call occurs during business hours or after hours, different actions (e.g., immediate follow-up or delayed messages) are triggered.

These workflows are set up to ensure that no missed calls go unanswered, providing a friendly and prompt response to potential clients and notifying the team internally for any necessary follow-up.
