Appointment Scheduling Push: The automation encourages the lead to schedule an appointment to redeem their certificate.

The funnel workflows are designed to continue nurturing the lead until they either book an appointment or engage with your practice.

6. Chiropractor Snapshot Documentation: 52-Week Nurture Campaign

The 52-Week Nurture Campaign consists of two fully automated workflows aimed at nurturing leads through regular touchpoints via both email and SMS over the course of a year. These workflows are designed to educate and build value while staying top-of-mind with leads to eventually turn them into paying clients.

1. 52-Week Email Nurture Campaign (Educational)

Overview:

This email campaign provides valuable, educational content to leads on a weekly basis, focusing on chiropractic care, health tips, and wellness advice.

Each email is designed to build a relationship with the lead, position the practice as an authority in chiropractic care, and encourage the recipient to take action (like booking an appointment or calling the practice).

Features:

Automated Emails: Each week, an educational email is sent to the lead. Topics range from stretches for back pain relief to boosting immunity through chiropractic care.

Wait Times: The system waits for seven days between each email to ensure consistent, but not overwhelming, communication.

Action-Oriented Messaging: Every email ends with a clear call-to-action, such as “Call to schedule an appointment” or “Reply for more information.”

Sample Emails:

Email 1: Boost Immunity with Chiropractic Care

Email 2: Simple Stretches for Back Pain Relief

Email 3: The Importance of Good Posture

...and 49 more emails over the course of the year.

2. 52-Week Text Message Nurture Campaign (Educational)

Overview:

Similar to the email campaign, this SMS nurture campaign is designed to provide educational tips and advice to leads in bite-sized text messages.

The messages are concise and action-oriented, encouraging leads to book appointments, visit the website, or reply for more information.

Features:

Automated SMS: Once a week, a text message is sent to the lead with a short tip or reminder about chiropractic care or general health.

Customizable Call-to-Actions: Each SMS contains a CTA like “Call us at {{location.phone}}” or “Reply YES to schedule a visit.”

Short and Simple Content: The messages are brief, ensuring they are easy to read and digest in a text message format.

Sample Messages:

Week 1: Did you know chiropractic care can boost your immune system? Give us a call to learn more {{location.phone}}! 😊

Week 2: Struggling with back pain? Here’s a quick stretch you can try at home: [link to blog]

Week 3: Proper posture is key to a healthy spine! Schedule an appointment to get a posture assessment. 📞{{location.phone}}

...and 49 more weekly SMS messages.

Automation:

Both the email and SMS nurture campaigns are fully automated, meaning that once a lead is enrolled, they will receive weekly content for an entire year.

These campaigns help to keep leads engaged, build trust over time, and eventually encourage them to convert into paying customers.

This nurture strategy is a powerful tool for chiropractor offices looking to maintain consistent communication with leads and provide long-term value that keeps the practice top-of-mind.

Additionally, an internal notification is sent to the business team to inform them of the missed call.

Trigger: Call Status = Missed Call

Automation Flow:

Check if the contact is new

If new, add the "Missed Call" tag

Send SMS with:

Message Example: "Hey there, this is John from {{location.name}}, we just got a missed call from this number, sorry about that! How can I help?"

Send an internal notification to notify the business team

2. Advanced Missed Call Text Back Sequence

This more advanced workflow is tailored for cases where there are multiple missed calls from the same lead. The response sequence changes based on how many times the lead called, whether during or after business hours.

Trigger: Call Status = Missed Call

Automation Flow:

Add the "1st Missed Call" tag

If a second or third missed call occurs, adjust the response and remove or update the tags

A delay of 2 minutes is added before sending SMS responses or internal notifications

The sequence branches based on business hours (AM/PM) and how many times the lead has called:

During business hours: SMS and internal notifications are sent based on a set time window

After business hours: SMS is sent, but the response is delayed for non-business hours

Notable Features:

Each call triggers a different response, ensuring that repeat leads get updated interactions.

If the caller contacts multiple times, the workflow escalates the follow-up process with enhanced internal notifications.

Both of these automations work together to ensure no lead is missed and proper follow-ups are in place based on how often the lead reaches out.
