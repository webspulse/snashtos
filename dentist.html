10. Google Review Campaign Documentation

Overview:

This campaign is designed to encourage customers to leave a Google review. It consists of a dedicated pipeline and two workflows.

Pipeline: Google Review Campaign 🌟

Stages:

Request Sent: The initial stage after a review request has been sent.

Review Link Clicked: The stage where the lead has clicked on the Google review link.

Not Clicked: The stage where the lead has not clicked on the review link.

Workflows:

Review Campaign:

Purpose: Sends a sequence of SMS and email messages to request a Google review from the customer.

Flow:

The workflow begins with updating the opportunity and sending the first SMS and email.

The system waits for a specific period and then sends subsequent SMS and email reminders if no action is taken.

The sequence continues until the customer either clicks the review link or the sequence is complete.

Review Campaign Link Clicked:

Purpose: Triggered when the customer clicks on the Google review link.

Flow:

This workflow sends an internal notification, updates the opportunity, tags the contact as "Review Link Clicked," and then removes the contact from the Review Campaign workflow to avoid sending further review requests.

This setup ensures a structured and automated approach to requesting and following up on Google reviews, improving the likelihood of customer engagement and feedback.

11. Inbound Lead Sources Automations Overview

The Inbound Lead Sources Automations are designed to ensure that leads reaching out through various channels receive timely and relevant responses based on the current status (open or closed) of the dental practice. These automations are integrated with the Dental Main Pipeline to efficiently manage and track leads from the initial point of contact through to booking appointments, following up, and nurturing.

Channels Supported

Facebook Messenger

Instagram Direct Messages (IG DM)

Google My Business (GMB)

SMS

Workflow Summary

Each lead source has a dedicated automation workflow that triggers based on the channel through which the lead initiates contact. The workflows are designed to:

Tag the Contact: Each contact is tagged according to the channel they used.

Create an Opportunity: An opportunity is created in the Dental Main Pipeline to track the lead's progress.

Determine Business Status: The workflow checks if the business is currently open or closed.

If Open: The lead receives a response indicating that the practice is open and available to assist them immediately.

If Closed: The lead receives an automated message informing them that the practice is currently closed, along with a promise to follow up as soon as the business reopens.

Pipeline Stages

All leads are funneled into the Dental Main Pipeline, which is structured to handle the various stages of a lead’s journey:

New Lead: The initial stage when a lead first reaches out.

Lead Responded: When a lead engages in a conversation or responds to the initial outreach.

Appointment Booked: When the lead schedules an appointment.

Appointment No Show: If the lead fails to attend the scheduled appointment.

Appointment Cancelled: If the lead cancels their appointment.

Follow Up: For leads that need further engagement or have shown interest but haven’t booked an appointment yet.

Not Interested: For leads that have declined services or expressed disinterest.

Notifications

Internal Notifications: Every time a lead reaches out, an internal notification is sent to the dental team to ensure they are aware of new inquiries and can follow up personally if needed.

Benefits

Consistent Lead Engagement: Ensures all leads are responded to promptly, regardless of the time or channel they use.

Efficient Pipeline Management: By integrating all lead sources into a single pipeline, the dental practice can easily track and manage leads at different stages.

Improved Conversion Rates: By maintaining consistent communication, leads are more likely to convert into booked appointments.

Streamlined Operations: The automations reduce the manual effort required to manage leads, allowing the dental team to focus on patient care.

Best Practices

Regular Monitoring: Ensure that the internal notifications are checked promptly by the team.

Updating Business Hours: Keep the business hours updated within the automation settings to avoid sending incorrect information to leads.

Customizing Responses: Tailor the automated messages to reflect the tone and branding of the dental practice.

This documentation should be used as a reference for setting up and managing the inbound lead sources automations within the Dental Main Pipeline. Proper implementation of these workflows will ensure a streamlined and efficient process for handling leads from various channels.

Overview

The Insurance Benefits May Be Expiring SMS Automation is designed to notify patients that their insurance benefits may be expiring soon, prompting them to schedule an appointment to take full advantage of their coverage. This automation is a simple yet effective reminder that encourages patients to act quickly to avoid losing their benefits.

Workflow Summary

Trigger: This automation is typically triggered manually or through a predetermined schedule (e.g., at the end of the calendar year or before a known expiration period).

Action:

Send SMS: The workflow sends a personalized SMS message to the patient.

Message Content:

The SMS message is designed to be direct and informative, reminding the patient of the potential expiration of their insurance benefits and urging them to schedule an appointment.

SMS Message Template

Message: Hi {{contact.first_name}}, your insurance benefits may be expiring soon. Don’t let them go to waste! Schedule your appointment today to take full advantage of your coverage. Call us at {{location.phone}} or book online.

Placeholders:

{{contact.first_name}}: Automatically inserts the patient's first name for personalization.

{{location.phone}}: Inserts the phone number of the dental practice.

Use Cases

End-of-Year Reminders: Send out this automation towards the end of the year when many insurance benefits are set to expire.

Quarterly Checks: Schedule this automation quarterly for patients who might have varying insurance periods or unused benefits.

Specific Campaigns: Use this SMS as part of a broader campaign to fill up appointment slots during slower periods.

Benefits

Timely Reminders: Ensures that patients are aware of the impending expiration of their insurance benefits, prompting them to schedule necessary treatments.

Increased Appointments: By highlighting the urgency, this SMS can help fill the calendar with appointments, especially towards the end of the insurance year.

Patient Satisfaction: Helps patients maximize their insurance benefits, improving overall satisfaction and loyalty to the dental practice.

Implementation Notes

Scheduling: Consider when to trigger this automation for maximum impact. The timing should align with common insurance expiration dates.

Message Personalization: Ensure the placeholders are correctly set up to personalize each message.

Tracking: Monitor the responses and bookings resulting from this SMS to measure its effectiveness.

Best Practices

Avoid Overuse: Use this SMS sparingly to avoid overwhelming patients with messages. It should be reserved for periods when benefits are most likely to expire.

Follow-Up: If a patient doesn't respond, consider a follow-up message or a call to ensure they received the information.

Compliance: Ensure that your messaging complies with any local regulations regarding communication, particularly around health insurance.

This documentation should serve as a reference for understanding and implementing the Insurance Benefits May Be Expiring SMS Automation within your practice’s workflow, ensuring timely and effective communication with patients.
